Who We Are

A Studio Built On Imagination

Stackly is a collective of illustrators, designers and animators obsessed with color, story and detail. We turn blank canvases into brand worlds people remember.

We blend art direction with craft to design visuals that launch brands forward.

From a single character sketch to a full multi-platform brand system, our team partners with startups and global companies to create artwork that stands out, scales and sells.

We start every project by understanding the story behind your brand. Then we sketch, iterate and refine until every pixel feels intentional — always on time, always on budget, and always with a spark of joy in the final piece.
